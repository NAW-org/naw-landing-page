7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • object to our use of it, where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • ask us to send it to you or another organisation in a portable format, where the law gives that right.

To use any of these rights, contact us at the address in section 1. Under the GDPR we reply within one month, which we may extend by two months for complex requests if we tell you within the first month.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. NAW legal division: today no row in Table 1 relies on consent. Delete this paragraph if that stays true.

8. Complaints

You can complain to us first, at the address in section 1. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority:

  • in the EU, the supervisory authority where you live or work, or where you think the problem happened. Our lead authority is the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus. NAW legal division: confirm the lead authority once the Cyprus company is registered.

9. Do you have to give us your data?

No. You can visit our websites without telling us who you are. If you email us, we need your email address to reply.
