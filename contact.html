Contact and support

Tell us when something is wrong. We want to hear about security problems, incidents, outages, anything that is not working as it should, and any concern or complaint you have about NAW.

Report a security issue or incident

Email security@usenaw.com

Use this address for:

  • a security vulnerability you found in our websites or services;
  • a suspected security incident, such as unauthorised access or a data leak;
  • a system problem or failure that affects you;
  • a suspected personal-data breach;
  • a phishing email or message that claims to come from NAW.

Partners, customers, suppliers and security researchers can all use it. The CTO and the DevSecOps lead read this mailbox. There is no penalty for a false alarm. If you are not sure, report it.

What to send

  • What you found, where, and how to reproduce it.
  • The date and time you noticed it.

We do not publish an encryption key, so do not put real partner or customer data in the email.

Our response targets

These are targets only. We acknowledge your report within 3 business days. We tell you whether we accept it, and at what severity, within 5 business days. We update you at least every 10 business days until it is fixed.

Vulnerability disclosure

In scope. usenaw.com, nawpn.com and anything served from them; the NAWPN corridor settlement service.

Out of scope. Partner bank systems, any custodian, and any third-party service we buy; denial of service, load testing and spam; social engineering of staff, partners or suppliers; physical attacks; scanner-only reports with no shown impact.

What we ask of you.

  1. Give us a fair chance to fix it before you tell anyone. We ask for 90 days, and we agree a shorter window if the fix ships sooner.
  2. Do not access, change or delete data that is not yours. If you reach real partner or customer data, stop and tell us what you saw.
  3. Do not degrade the service for anyone else.
  4. Keep what you find confidential until we agree it can be published.

Safe harbour. We will not take legal action against you for research that follows this policy. This does not cover you against a third party whose systems you touch, and it does not waive anyone else's rights.

No bug bounty. We do not pay for reports. We credit you by name if you want, or keep you anonymous.

Our security contact is also published in machine-readable form at /.well-known/security.txt.
